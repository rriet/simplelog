Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 28, 2026

Overview

This application is a free and open-source app developed by RietLabs. Your privacy is respected by design: the app does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer.

Information Collection

This app does not collect any personal data from users.

  • No account creation is required
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used
  • No personal information is gathered or stored remotely

Data Storage

All information created or used within the app remains on your device.

  • Data is not sent to external servers operated by RietLabs
  • Users retain full control over their data

Internet Usage

The app may use an internet connection for the following purposes:

  • Opening map services (such as OpenStreetMap)
  • Synchronizing data between a user’s own devices

During these processes:

  • Data is transmitted only between the user’s devices or to third-party services required for functionality
  • No data is transmitted to or stored by RietLabs

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with third-party services (e.g., map providers like OpenStreetMap) to provide functionality.

These services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

RietLabs does not:

  • Sell user data
  • Share user data with third parties
  • Store user data on external servers

Security

Because all data is stored locally and not transmitted to developer-controlled servers, the risk of centralized data exposure is minimized.

However, users are responsible for securing their own devices.

Open Source

This app is open source, meaning its code can be inspected publicly. This provides transparency into how data is handled.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
